Outpipe allows you to serve tunnels from your own branded domain names (e.g., `api.yourcompany.com` or `preview.internal.dev`) instead of default `*.outpipe.app` subdomains.

## Adding a Custom Domain

1. In the **Outpipe Dashboard**, navigate to **Domains** and click **+ Add Domain**.
2. Enter your fully qualified domain name (e.g., `api.example.com`).
3. Outpipe generates unique verification records for your domain.

## DNS Verification

You can verify ownership using either **CNAME** or **TXT** records in your DNS provider (Cloudflare, Route53, Namecheap, etc.):

### Option A: CNAME Record (Recommended)
Point your subdomain directly to the Outpipe relay hostname:
- **Type**: `CNAME`
- **Name**: `api` (or your subdomain)
- **Target**: `cname.outpipe.app`

### Option B: TXT Verification Record
Add a TXT record to prove ownership before pointing traffic:
- **Type**: `TXT`
- **Name**: `_outpipe-challenge.api.example.com`
- **Value**: `outpipe-verify=<verification_token>`

## Automated SSL/TLS Certificates (Let's Encrypt)

Once verified, Outpipe automatically provisions and manages free SSL/TLS certificates via Let's Encrypt (ACME). 

- **Automatic Renewal**: Certificates are renewed in the background 30 days before expiration.
- **Zero-Downtime Reloads**: The relay dynamically reloads certificates in memory without dropping active WebSocket or HTTP tunnel connections.

## Connecting with the CLI

Once your domain is active and verified, start your tunnel specifying your managed tunnel ID or custom subdomain:

```sh
outpipe http --port 3000 --tunnel-id <your_tunnel_id>
```
